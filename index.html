FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

A few of the questions we hear most often before a cleaning is scheduled.

Do you offer one-time and recurring cleaning?

Yes. We offer one-time cleaning as well as recurring residential and commercial cleaning plans.

Do you clean both homes and businesses?

Yes. No Trace Cleaning provides residential cleaning for homes and commercial cleaning for offices and local businesses.

How do I get a quote?

Send a quote request through the site. We'll review the details of your space and contact you to discuss the cleaning and next steps.

Can I choose how often my home is cleaned?

Yes. Recurring residential plans can be discussed around the schedule that works for your home, including weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or a custom schedule.

What areas do you serve?

We focus on Baton Rouge and surrounding communities. If you're unsure whether your location is within our service area, send a quote request and we'll confirm availability.

How does the recurring residential offer work?

Eligible new recurring residential customers can ask about 50% off their first deep clean. Final eligibility, scope, pricing, and availability are confirmed in the quote.
